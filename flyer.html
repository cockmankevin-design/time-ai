Pays for Itself Free for Students

Time AI helps college students track volunteer hours, build daily habits, manage sleep, and get personalized time management coaching. Nonprofits that need help get boosted. Students never pay a cent.

🤝 Volunteer Tracking
Discover local opportunities, log hours with a built-in timer or manual entry, and climb the leaderboard.
✅ Daily Habits
Build streaks for studying, exercise, hydration, reading, and custom goals. Visual progress tracking.
🌙 Sleep Tracking
Log bedtime and wake time, rate quality, and see weekly trends with color-coded charts and averages.
💬 Smart Time Coach
Get personalized study tips, stress management, day planning, and motivation from an always-available coach.
