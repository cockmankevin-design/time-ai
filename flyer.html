The Student Volunteer Platform That
Pays for Itself Free for Students

Time AI helps college students track volunteer hours, build daily habits, manage sleep, and get personalized time management coaching. Nonprofits that need help get boosted. Students never pay a cent.

🤝 Volunteer Tracking
Discover local opportunities, log hours with a built-in timer or manual entry, and climb the leaderboard against other students.
✅ Daily Habits
Build streaks for studying, exercise, hydration, reading, and custom goals. Visual progress tracking keeps you accountable.
🌙 Sleep Tracking
Log bedtime and wake time, rate sleep quality, and see weekly trends with color-coded charts, averages, and goal tracking.
💬 Smart Time Coach
Get personalized study tips, stress management strategies, day planning, and motivation from an always-available coach.
📣

Community Boost: Helping Nonprofits That Need It Most

Organizations low on volunteers get automatically promoted to the top of the feed with a "Needs Help" badge and a live volunteer progress bar. Sponsored listing revenue funds these free boosts, so the platform's business model directly drives social impact.
